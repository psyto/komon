📍 WHAT IS KOMON?
Komon (コモン, from "commons") is a protocol where anyone can:
• Post civic problems in their community
• Propose solutions ("directions")
• Stake money on which solutions will work
• Get rewarded when they're right

No crypto knowledge needed. You see dollars. We handle the rest.

⚡ THE TRANSFORMATION
Traditional prediction market: "Will the city fix potholes this year?"
Komon: "Which approach will fix THIS pothole?"

The first generates information. The second generates actionable intelligence.

• Web3 invisible to users — wallet abstraction
